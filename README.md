# Jan Malmström

Founder of **[Simaroa](https://simaroa.com)**, a speed-to-lead and quoting platform for US cleaning and home-service businesses.

Small service businesses rarely lose jobs on price. They lose because a competitor answered first. Simaroa gives every new lead an instant alert, a one-click quote and automated follow-up.

After 20 years in customer service at DHL, I build software with AI. Simaroa went from first commit to live product in about five weeks.

## Currently building

- **Simaroa**: multi-tenant SaaS platform (Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, React, Docker), live in English and Spanish
- **[lupaph-mcp](https://github.com/janmalmstrom/lupaph-mcp)**: MCP server for Philippine real estate, with verified listings, transfer costs and legal rules
- **[local-contractor-mcp](https://github.com/janmalmstrom/local-contractor-mcp)**: MCP server for finding local contractors
